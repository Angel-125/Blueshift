            
Renders a visual-only Graviolium impact spray at a caller-supplied pose.
        
## Properties

### IsPlaying
Indicates whether the starburst is emitting.
## Methods


### Play(UnityEngine.Vector3,UnityEngine.Vector3,Blueshift.GravioliumStarburstSettings)
Starts emitting at a world-space position. The normal is perpendicular to the radial spray plane and should point from the source toward the impact.

### SetPose(UnityEngine.Vector3,UnityEngine.Vector3)
Updates the world-space impact position and spray-plane normal.

### StopEffect
Stops emission and immediately removes all living particles.
